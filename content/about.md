Hi, I'm **xiangxin**, known as **Schofi** on GitHub.

This is my personal blog. It's a place to collect ideas from reading, experiments, and engineering work, and turn scattered notes into articles I can revisit, discuss, and refine.

My interests include:

- **LLM post-training**: how further training makes models more useful.
- **Agentic RL**: bringing reinforcement learning and agents together.
- **Agent infrastructure**: making agent systems easier to build, evaluate, and improve.
- **Industrial AI**: applying models and engineering methods to real-world problems.

I aim to explain the context behind each problem, walk through my reasoning, and include references. When an idea is still taking shape, I'll be clear about what remains uncertain.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Schofi) or subscribe to updates via [RSS](/index.xml).
